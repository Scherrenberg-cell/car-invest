About the tool

The Car Investment Estimator covers 1,340 vehicles across 63 marques (€3,490–€48 M). It produces a condition- and mileage-adjusted market value, a three-scenario projected net return, and a composite Investment Score for every vehicle — all calibrated against real auction data from RM Sotheby's, Bonhams, Hagerty and Classic.com.

Scoring revised June 2026, grounded in hedonic pricing literature (Le Fur 2023, Knight Frank KFLII 2026, Masset & Weisskopf 2018).

Key assumptions
  • Base values represent a typical Good-condition market price cross-referenced against real auction records. They are mid-market estimates, not asking prices.
  • Appreciation rates are brand-level benchmarks calibrated on Hagerty Price Guide 5-year CAGR data and Classic.com 10-year trend indices.
  • 50 Trophy-Asset CAGR overrides are applied for ultra-rare vehicles (Ferrari 250 GTO, Aston DB4 GT Zagato, etc.) where individually researched rates replace the formula.
  • Ownership costs range 2.5–8.5% of value per annum and are fixed per brand. They do not vary with restoration state or storage location.
  • Brand Prestige scores (new in v6.3) are point estimates derived from KFLII 2026 and auction sell-through data. They will shift as generational demand evolves.
  • Tax schedules reflect published 2025/2026 rates. All three country regimes are subject to legislative change.
⚠ Potential pitfalls
  • Not an appraisal. All outputs are directional planning estimates. Individual auction results vary enormously based on specification, provenance documentation and vendor reputation.
  • Condition × mileage interaction. Applying Excellent (×1.15) and very low mileage (×1.32) together may overstate values for pristine survivors — the factors are independent but correlated in reality.
  • Thin markets. Rare cars (rarity ≥ 8) trade infrequently; one distressed sale can move apparent market value by 30–40%. The Volatility score attempts to flag this but cannot prevent it.
  • Pre-war penalty. The revised era modifier (−0.3pp for pre-1950 cars) reflects current generational demand research. It may understate values for exceptional racing-provenance pre-war cars not covered by the Trophy-Asset override list.
  • No momentum guarantee. Bull/Bear momentum flags are qualitative assessments based on current market direction and may lag or lead actual price movements by 12–18 months.
  • Currency risk. Base values are in EUR. Auction markets are USD and GBP dominated; exchange-rate moves create real divergence from these estimates for non-EUR investors.
